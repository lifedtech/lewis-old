What is LEWIS

A reflection, not a verdict.

LEWIS, the Lifed Everyday Wellbeing Intelligence System, is Lifed’s non-clinical wellbeing reflection tool. It understands how life feels for you right now, reflects it back across nine connected areas, then names one small place to start.

Questions, tasks and activities

A body map, a breathing minute, a sound check, a few quick choices. What you do counts as much as what you say.

Nine areas, read together

LEWIS sees which areas are moving together and where a small effort reaches furthest. Leverage, never a claim about cause.

It starts small

What comes back begins with one habit small enough to actually do. Support always comes before anything else.

The science and research

What LEWIS is built on.

Established self-report research where it exists, and honesty about the parts that are reflection rather than measurement.

Validated questions carry the weight

Every area is anchored to established, peer-reviewed self-report research. That wording is never altered, in any language, because that is what makes it mean anything.

Tasks and activities only nudge

The behavioural moments are suggestive signals. They can shade a reading within a tight bound when they agree with it, and can never create a problem on their own.

Traceable, not generated

Everything LEWIS tells you maps back to your own answers and efforts. Where the picture is thin, it says so rather than guessing.

LEWIS makes no claim about hormones, brain states or heart rhythms. It does not detect, name, treat or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for professional care.

What is LEWIS?

LEWIS, the Lifed Everyday Wellbeing Intelligence System, is Lifed’s non-clinical wellbeing reflection tool. Through questions, tasks and guided activities, it understands how life feels for you right now and reflects it back across nine connected areas of everyday wellbeing. It takes 12 to 15 minutes, and it does not diagnose.

How long does LEWIS take?

12 to 15 minutes, taken at your own pace in one sitting.

What does LEWIS cost?

Starting LEWIS costs nothing. Your complete report, around 20 pages, is ₹399.

Is it only questions?

No. Alongside the questions there are short tasks and guided activities, including a body map, a breathing minute, a sound check and a few quick choices. What you do in them adds to the picture as much as what you answer.

Is LEWIS a medical or mental health assessment?

No. LEWIS is non-clinical. It does not diagnose, treat or measure any condition, and it is not a replacement for professional care. It is a structured reflection, not a verdict.

What are the nine areas of wellbeing?

Physical & Regeneration, Emotional & Mental, Engagement & Energy, Relationships & Social, Meaning & Spiritual, Accomplishment & Agency, Financial Wellness, Intellectual & Growth, and Environmental & Cultural.

What is the research behind LEWIS?

Each of the nine areas is anchored to established, peer-reviewed self-report research, and the wording of those questions is never altered. The tasks and activities alongside them can shade a reading only when they agree with it, never create one.

Who can use LEWIS, and do I need a wearable?

LEWIS is for adults aged 18 and over, and it confirms your age before it begins. No wearable or connected device is involved. LEWIS works from your own answers and efforts, and makes no claims about hormones, brain states or heart-rate patterns.
