All marks are drawn on an 82 unit artboard, no embedded raster, no clipping
masks. Scale freely.

Type
  Manrope ExtraBold 800 for the wordmark, tracking -2.5%
  Nunito for everything the reader reads. Bold 700 at 24% tracking for BY LIFED

Minimum sizes
  16px favicon, 24px screen, 40px with wordmark, 8mm print.
  Below 24px drop the two small counterpoint dots (use lewis-mark-simplified.svg).

Clear space
  One cell, 22 units, or 27% of the mark width, on every side.
